# Objection FAQ
## Short answers a salesman can say aloud

Rule: stay calm. Agree with the concern. Then give the plain answer. Never invent endorsements.

---

## Security and privacy

**Objection:** "Where does our data go? Is this safe?"

**Say:**

> Fair question. In a pilot, data handling follows your ministry IT and legal path. We put that in the MoU. Humans control what becomes official. We do not sell personal data. Your counsel should review the template before anyone signs.

**Do not say:** detailed server brand names in a non-technical room unless IT asks in a separate session.

---

## French vs Somali / Afar

**Objection:** "Our people speak Somali and Afar. Will this work?"

**Say:**

> French is first and strongest for official-style drafts today. Somali and Afar quality is improving. We will not claim it is perfect. In the pilot we score language quality with your staff and fix what matters most.

---

## Will AI replace counsellors or officers?

**Objection:** "Are you trying to replace our people?"

**Say:**

> No. The tool drafts. Your counsellor or officer reviews and submits. If the draft is wrong, the human fixes it. We are buying time back for placement and casework, not removing judgment.

---

## Internet down

**Objection:** "Wifi is bad here. What happens when the network drops?"

**Say:**

> Real issue in many meeting rooms and field offices. For demos we use hotspot and a backup recording. For pilots we agree offline and poor-network expectations with your IT in week one. We do not pretend every site has perfect wifi.

---

## Cost

**Objection:** "How much?"

**Say:**

> This ask is a four-week paid pilot with ten users, not a multi-year mega-contract on day one. Exact fee sits in the MoU annex after we know scope. We can walk numbers with the sponsor and finance once the problem and success metrics are clear.

If pressed for a number you do not have:

> I will not invent a price in the room. I will send a pilot fee range with the MoU draft within one business day after we confirm scope.

---

## Why not WhatsApp?

**Objection:** "Why not just use WhatsApp?"

**Say:**

> WhatsApp is great for messages. It is weak for structured ministry files, field-by-field review, PDF outputs, and audit trails. We are not asking people to abandon WhatsApp for chat. We are solving the counsellor or officer file step.

---

## ANC / Code numérique / digital law concerns

**Objection:** "Our digital rules say humans stay responsible. Voice can be sensitive."

**Say:**

> We agree. Human decides. Voice may be sensitive, so your ministry sets what can be recorded and where. We follow ministry IT and legal instructions in the pilot. Nothing is official until an authorized person submits.

---

## Competing with Djibouti Emploi portal

**Objection:** "We already have Djibouti Emploi. Why do we need you?"

**Say:**

> We are not a competing jobs portal. Emploi Assist voice-enables the counsellor step on Djibouti Emploi logic. The portal stays the portal. We help the interview become a clean file faster, with human review.

---

## "Show us a ministry that already signed"

**Objection:** "Who already uses this officially?"

**Say:**

> I will not invent endorsements. We are here for a careful pilot with a named sponsor. Warm forum interest is real. A signature is earned with a four-week proof, not with a fake logo slide.

---

## "Can it connect to our live systems on day one?"

**Objection:** "We want live API to the national portal immediately."

**Say:**

> Not the pilot promise. Pilot proves the counsellor or officer workflow with human review. Live system connections are a later IT decision under your rules, in writing.

---

## "Somali/Afar must be perfect or we refuse"

**Say:**

> Then we should measure quality in week one and decide together. Overclaiming helps nobody. French-first pilot with language scoring is the honest path.

---

## "We need exclusivity"

**Say:**

> Default pilot has no exclusivity. If you need exclusivity, we negotiate it in an annex. It is not automatic.

---

## Quick recovery line (any hard objection)

> You are protecting your institution. That is good. Tell me the concern in one sentence, and we will either solve it in the MoU or admit we cannot. Which is it for you?
