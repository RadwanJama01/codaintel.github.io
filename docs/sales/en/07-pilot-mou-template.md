## 2. Purpose

The Parties agree to run a limited **paid pilot** so Party A can test whether the Product helps staff turn spoken work into structured files **with human review before anything is treated as official**.

This MoU does not transfer ownership of ministry data. It does not create a permanent procurement award unless a later contract says so.

- **Start Date:** _______________
- **End Date:** _______________

## 4. Roles

### Party A (Ministry / Agency)

- Name a **Sponsor** (single accountable person): _______________
- Nominate up to **ten (10) trained users**.
- Provide reasonable access to a test workflow (no requirement to connect live national production systems unless separately agreed in writing).
- Decide what counts as an "official" record under Party A rules. The Product drafts. Humans submit.
- Involve IT / security / legal as Party A requires.

### Party B (Phoenix Rising Group)

- Provide pilot access to the Product for named users.
- Deliver short user training (format agreed in week one).
- Support during business hours agreed in Annex A (time zone: Africa/Djibouti / as agreed).
- Collect only the pilot metrics listed in Section 7, unless Party A approves more in writing.
- Do not publicly claim Party A endorsement without written approval.

---

## 5. Data handling

- Pilot data remains under **Party A control** as defined by Party A IT and legal instructions.
- Party B processes pilot data only to run the pilot and improve the pilot experience with Party A consent.
- No sale of personal data.
- Sensitive voice content may require extra Party A rules. Party A may restrict recording locations, languages, or case types.
- At End Date (or earlier on request), Party B will follow the exit steps in Section 9 for data return or deletion as Party A directs, subject to mandatory law.

**Note for sales conversations:** say "we follow ministry IT." Do not invent hosting locations in this template unless counsel fills them in.

---

## 6. Commercial terms and exclusivity

- This is a **paid pilot**. Fee, invoice schedule, and payment method: see **Annex B** (to be completed).
- **No exclusivity** unless Annex C is signed. Either Party may speak with others during the pilot unless Annex C says otherwise.
- Intellectual property in Party B pre-existing software stays with Party B.
- Party A retains rights in its data, forms, and ministry content.

## 8. Human oversight (non-negotiable product rule)

Nothing generated by the Product is an official ministry record until an authorized human reviews and submits under Party A rules.

---

## 9. Exit

At End Date, or on 7 days written notice for material breach:

1. Access for pilot users ends on the agreed date.
2. Party A receives export of pilot records in an agreed format, if technically available.
3. Party B deletes or returns Party A pilot personal data as Party A directs, except copies required by law.
4. Each Party returns or destroys the other Party's confidential materials on request.
5. Sections on confidentiality, data, and IP survive as counsel specifies in the final legal version.
