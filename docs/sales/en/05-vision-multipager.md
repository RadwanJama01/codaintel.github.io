# Vision brief
## One documentation pipe, many ministry tenants

**Phoenix Rising Group** | English | 3 to 5 pages spoken length  
**Audience:** ministry sponsors, chiefs of staff, IT advisors sitting in sales meetings

---

## Page 1. The vision in plain words

Djibouti ministries already run on documents: jobseeker files, statements, declarations, reports.

The bottleneck is not "more software." It is the gap between **what people say** and **what the file needs**.

Our vision is simple:

> **One documentation pipe. Many ministry tenants.**

Same pipe. Different logos, users, and rules per ministry.

### The pipe (memorize this)

1. **Audio** (or notes) comes in.
2. **Transcript** is produced.
3. A **draft file** is structured into fields.
4. A **human reviews** and edits.
5. The file becomes an **official record** only after human submit.
6. An **audit trail** keeps who changed what and when.

That is the whole product idea. Everything else is packaging for a ministry.

---

## Page 2. Why Djibouti now

Three reasons, none of them invented endorsements:

1. **AI forum warmth.** Decision-makers are curious and open. Curiosity is not a signed contract. It is a door.
2. **Digitization push.** Ministries want cleaner files, less retyping, better follow-up.
3. **Human oversight norms.** Djibouti's digital and legal conversation (including Code numérique style expectations) stresses that people remain responsible. Our design matches that: human decides, system drafts.

We do not claim the State has chosen us. We claim the timing is right for a careful pilot.

### What "tenant" means in plain English

A tenant is a separate ministry workspace. Separate users. Separate forms. Separate branding. Shared pipe underneath.

We do **not** open a new ministry tenant because it looks cool. We open it when a sponsor asks and owns the pilot.

## Page 4. How it helps Djiboutians

### Jobs and dignity (Emploi Assist)

- Counsellors spend less time fighting French forms and more time placing people.
- Structured skills data helps match people to real openings.
- Cleaner files support pathways toward formal work and social protection when the ministry and CNSS processes require declarations.
- Youth who are outside work and training need systems that meet them in speech, not only in long written French.

### Safety and time (VoiceDoc)

- Officers finish statements faster.
- Fewer copy errors between notebook and typed file.
- Audit trails support supervision and trust.
- People can speak languages they use every day. French remains the strongest official draft path today. Somali and Afar quality is improving and must be measured honestly.

### Shared public value

- Less lost paper.
- Clearer accountability.
- Staff keep their jobs and judgment. Tools draft. People decide.

## Page 5. Trust and governance

### Hard rules we sell

1. **Human review before official.** Always.
2. **Audit trail.** Who edited. Who submitted. When.
3. **Ministry control in pilots.** Data handling follows the ministry IT and legal path agreed in the MoU.
4. **No fake endorsements.** Warm meetings are not signatures.
5. **Honest language quality.** French first. Local languages improving. Measure in pilot.

### What we will not do in a pitch

- Promise unsupervised AI decisions.
- Promise a live national portal replacement.
- Promise perfect Somali/Afar today.
- Invent statistics or queue times we do not have.

### Pilot framing (closing paragraph you can read)

> Start small. Four weeks. Named sponsor. Ten trained users. Agree metrics in week one. Keep humans in charge. If it works, expand. If it does not, exit cleanly. That is how we earn trust in Djibouti.
