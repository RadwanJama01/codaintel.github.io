VoiceDoc

One-pager for Djibouti National Police (DNP)


Phoenix Rising Group | Officer drafting tool | Pilot framing



The problem


Officers lose time handwriting statements, then retyping them into a clean file.


  • Errors appear when the same facts are copied twice.
  • Handwritten notes are hard to search later.
  • There is often no clear audit trail of who changed what and when.
  • Time spent on paperwork is time not spent on the case.


    • What it is (one sentence)


    VoiceDoc lets an officer record a spoken statement, get a structured draft, edit it, and submit a PDF with an audit trail.


    A human always reviews before anything is treated as official.



    Who uses it


    RoleUses it?
    Police officer / statement takerYes. Primary user.
    Supervisor / reviewerYes. Checks drafts and audit.
    Public / complainant self-serviceNo. Staff tool.

    Human in the loop (always)


    1. Officer records voice.

    2. System drafts structured fields.

    3. Officer (or reviewer) edits.

    4. Officer submits.

    5. Audit trail keeps who did what.


    If the draft is wrong, the human fixes it. That is the product, not a bug.



    Languages


  • French first. Strongest path for forms and official drafts today.
  • Somali and Afar: quality is improving. Useful for capture. Do not claim world-best or finished accuracy.
  • Be honest in the room: "French is ready for pilot. Local languages are getting better. We will measure quality with your officers."


    • What we are NOT


  • Not a replacement for police judgment.
  • Not an unsupervised system that files cases alone.
  • Not a promise of perfect Somali/Afar today.


    • Pilot ask (say this aloud)


    Four-week paid pilot. Named sponsor inside DNP. Ten trained users. Every report reviewed by a human before it is official. Success metrics agreed up front.
