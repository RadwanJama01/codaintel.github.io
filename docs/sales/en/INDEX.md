# English sales kit index
## Reading order for the salesman

**Audience:** non-technical salesman after a warm AI forum in Djibouti.  
**Lead products:** Emploi Assist (MoL / ANEFIP) and VoiceDoc (DNP).  
**Site pack note:** materials may later live at `/docs/sales/` on phoenixrisinggroups.com (**noindex until Radwan OK**). Until then, use this folder.

---

### Day-0 prep (night before)

1. `03-salesman-talk-track.md` - memorize 60-second elevator + your track (MoL or Police)
2. `04-demo-cheat-sheet.md` - practice the proven click path twice
3. `01-emploi-one-pager.md` and/or `02-voicedoc-one-pager.md` - know the one-sentence pitch
4. `08-objection-faq.md` - skim hard objections (portal competition, WhatsApp, replace staff, privacy)
5. `06-metrics-brief.md` - memorize only the top figures you will say aloud; say "published estimate"

### Morning of meeting

6. `09-pitch-deck.md` - if using slides, rehearse notes out loud
7. `05-vision-multipager.md` - only if a chief of staff wants the longer story
8. Confirm demo logins: `0001` / `changeme`
   - Emploi: https://voicedoc-emploi.vercel.app
   - VoiceDoc: https://dnp-voiceai.vercel.app
9. Hotspot ready. Backup screen recording ready.

### In the room

10. Talk track open / pain / demo / ask
11. Demo cheat sheet beside the laptop
12. Closing ask: 4-week paid pilot, named sponsor, 10 trained users

### Follow-up (same day / next day)

13. `10-leave-behind-checklist.md` - email pack + CRM fields
14. `07-pilot-mou-template.md` - send only as template for counsel review

### Optional depth / IT

15. Vision IT appendix (inside `05-vision-multipager.md`) + infra one-liner in talk track
16. Landscape source (internal): `../sources/landscape-mol-dnp-brief-2026-09-15.md`
