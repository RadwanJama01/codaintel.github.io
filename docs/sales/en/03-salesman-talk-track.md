# Salesman talk track
## Speakable scripts for Djibouti ministry meetings

**Audience:** non-technical salesman after a warm AI forum.  
**Rule:** short sentences. No jargon. Human always reviews.

---

## 60-second elevator (memorize)

> After the forum, many ministries asked the same question: how do we use AI without losing control?
>
> We built a simple pipe. Someone speaks. The system drafts a file. A counsellor or an officer reviews it. Then they submit. Nothing is official until a human says yes.
>
> For Labor, that is Emploi Assist. The ANEFIP counsellor runs the interview by voice. The jobseeker file fills in. The counsellor checks and submits.
>
> For Police, that is VoiceDoc. The officer records the statement. Gets a structured draft. Edits. Submits a PDF with an audit trail.
>
> We are not replacing Djibouti Emploi. We are not replacing officers. We want a four-week paid pilot with a named sponsor and ten trained users.
>
> Can we schedule that conversation this week?

---

## Infra one-liner (memorize; use only if asked "how does it work?")

> Each product is a simple website. When someone records, audio goes to our always-on service, which turns speech into form fields. Staff always review and edit before submit or export. Emploi and VoiceDoc share that same setup. Only the forms differ. We show demo sites only, never the service links.

## 5-minute ministry meeting script

### Open (45 seconds)

> Thank you for the time. I am here from Phoenix Rising Group.
>
> You saw the AI forum energy. Our ask is practical, not flashy.
>
> We help your staff turn spoken work into clean files, with a human review before anything is official.
>
> Today I will show one product in five minutes, then ask for a short pilot.

### Pain (60 seconds)

**If MoL / ANEFIP:**

> Counsellors interview in speech. Forms are in French. Informal work is large. Youth outside work and training is large. Retyping wastes time and creates errors. Placement capacity is already stretched.

**If Police:**

> Officers write by hand, then retype. Errors happen. Search is hard. Audit trails are weak. Paperwork eats case time.

### Demo (2 minutes)

> I will not show code. I will click the path your staff would use.
>
> Login. New interview or new incident. Speak or paste. Fields fill. Edit one field on purpose. Submit. Export if shown. Then My interviews or My reports.
>
> Watch for one thing only: the human can change every field before submit.

(Use `04-demo-cheat-sheet.md` for exact clicks.)

### Ask (45 seconds)

> We propose a four-week paid pilot.
>
> We need three things from you:
> 1. A named sponsor.
> 2. Ten users to train.
> 3. Agreement on how we measure success in week one.
>
> Data stays under your control for the pilot. No exclusivity unless we negotiate it later.
>
> Who should own this internally, and when can we meet them?

## Closing ask (use every time)

> Four-week paid pilot. Named sponsor. Ten trained users. Human review on every file. Success metrics set in week one. Can we put names and dates on paper this week?

If they hesitate:

> Fair. What would make a pilot feel safe for your IT and legal teams? We can walk the MoU draft with your counsel. It is a template, not legal advice.
