Emploi Assist

One-pager for Ministry of Labor / ANEFIP


Phoenix Rising Group | For counsellor use only | Pilot framing



The problem


Job counsellors spend too much time fighting forms instead of placing people.


  • Interviews are spoken. Files are written in French.
  • Informal work is about 52.7% of employment (WB DEM Spring 2026). Many jobseekers have little formal schooling.
  • Youth NEET is about 46.2% (WB DEM Spring 2026). Skills and vacancies often do not match.
  • Counsellors retype what they just heard. Errors creep in. Follow-up slows down.

    • (Sources for figures: see Metrics Brief. Treat as published estimates, not Phoenix Rising measurements.)



    What it is (one sentence)


    Emploi Assist turns a spoken counsellor interview into a structured jobseeker file. The counsellor reviews every field, then submits.


    Nothing becomes official until a human says so.



    Who uses it


    RoleUses it?
    ANEFIP counsellorYes. Primary user.
    JobseekerSpeaks in the interview. Does not operate the tool.
    Public kiosk / walk-in self-serviceNo. This is not a kiosk product.

    Why Minister Yonis / MoL cares


    1. Placement: Faster, cleaner dossiers mean counsellors can spend more time matching people to jobs.

    2. Skills matching: Structured fields make it easier to see what someone can actually do.

    3. Formalization path: Cleaner files support declare-the-worker / CNSS-style next steps when the ministry chooses that workflow. We do not replace CNSS. We help the counsellor step that feeds it.
