The last mile

The file is incomplete. Placement still stalls.

Informal work, youth out of education and work, and skills mismatch put pressure on public employment services. Registration is not placement. The bottleneck is often last-mile: a counsellor cannot get a complete, reviewable file from the conversation in the room.

Incomplete dossiers

CVs, diplomas, languages, and licenses never land in one place. Matching starts from a partial file.

Registration is not placement

A person can be on a list and still wait. Without a complete, structured profile, matching stalls.

French-first forms freeze people out

The conversation in the room is often Somali, Afar, or Arabic. The form is French. What was said does not become the file.

Formalization is a second paper chase

After a match, CNSS “declare the worker” paperwork starts over — another incomplete dossier, on a different desk.

Fragmented tools

Counsellors juggle portals that do not talk to each other. The interview, the file, and the declaration live in different systems.

What it does

One interview. A reviewable file. A draft path to declaration.

Built for employment counsellors

A staffed intake conversation, not a public kiosk and not a self-serve jobseeker portal. The counsellor conducts the interview and remains responsible for the record.

Alongside portals, not instead of them

Same pipe, labour schema

VoiceDoc’s loop — voice, transcript, structured draft, human review — applied to a jobseeker file and a formal-declaration draft. The schema changes; the review step does not.

Workflow

Voice to a signed-off file.

The counsellor stays with the jobseeker from the first question through export. The system drafts fields. It does not submit them, place anyone, or declare a worker.

  1. 01 · Voice interview

    The counsellor conducts and records the conversation in the language spoken in the room.

  2. 02 · Transcript

    The recording is transcribed. The audio is kept so a reviewer can hear what the draft claims.

  3. 03 · Structured file

    The transcript is mapped onto a jobseeker profile — skills, experience, languages, credentials, availability.

  4. 04 · Human review

    Every field is editable. Nothing is complete until a counsellor has reviewed and signed off.

  5. 05 · Export and draft path

    After review: a structured export, plus a CNSS-ready “declare the worker” draft. Not an auto-filed declaration.
