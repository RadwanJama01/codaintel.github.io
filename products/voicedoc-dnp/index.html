Voice documentation · Government & public sector

VoiceDoc

A mobile-first, offline-capable voice documentation platform for field agencies operating in low-connectivity environments.

Overview

Record a statement, review a structured draft, submit.

VoiceDoc is built for desks that have to stand behind a file: a field officer records a statement, an AI pipeline transcribes and structures it into fixed incident or PV-style fields, and the officer reviews and edits every field before anything is submitted. Nothing is auto-filed. The model drafts; the person at the desk decides.

Offline-first

Installs as a mobile app (PWA) and works without connectivity. Recordings and drafts queue locally and sync automatically once a connection returns, with idempotent submission so a retried sync can’t double-file a report.

Human-in-the-loop by design

Every AI-structured field is editable before submission. Reports, exports, and identifiers only exist once a person has explicitly reviewed and signed off.

Audit trail

Every view, create, edit, and export of a record is logged and reconstructable. Records are never hard-deleted, only tombstoned, so provenance survives independent of a record’s current state.

Workflow

Built for multilingual field conditions.

Statements are recorded in whatever language the person speaks. Where the underlying speech model has reliable coverage, a transcript and structured draft are generated automatically. Where it does not — including several Horn of Africa languages with little or no existing ASR coverage — the statement is flagged for human transcription rather than guessed, and the audio is preserved either way.

Graceful degradation

If the AI pipeline fails or times out, the recording and case record are still saved cleanly with empty fields for manual completion. A model outage never costs a record.

Role- and unit-scoped access

Access to case data is scoped server-side by operational unit, not just hidden in the interface, so different teams only ever see the records relevant to their function.

Case management dashboard

Supervisors get filtering, search, status workflows, and export across cases, with the same audit guarantees as the field-facing app.

Export-ready

Structured reports export to PDF with organization-specific formatting, ready for records systems and downstream review.
