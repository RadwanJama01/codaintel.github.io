What

A filled-in weight form.

The driver states the load. The agent records a truck weight check (contrôle de poids). The form fills in. It stays a draft.

Truck, cargo, weight

The spoken check lands in the boxes: truck, cargo, and weight.

You talk. It writes the file.

The agent does not type the form from scratch.

A person checks it

The agent reads every line and fixes it before anything is saved.

Who

The agent at the weigh station.

The agent records the check and remains responsible for the file. The driver speaks. The agent decides what is saved.

How

Record. Draft. Review. Export.

  1. 01 Record You talk at the desk.
  2. 02 Draft You talk. It writes the file.
  3. 03 Review A person reads every line.
  4. 04 Export Marked DRAFT until a person signs off.

Export prints « Projet, soumis à validation ». That means marked DRAFT until a person signs off. A draft, not a stamped official document. Early demo. Pilot only. No ministry has adopted this.
