From spoken language to official-style forms.

Langues nationales. Services publics. Humains dans la boucle.

Phoenix Rising is the language layer for Djibouti and Horn of Africa public-service last mile. Spoken Somali, Afar, Arabic, and French become a structured draft an officer or counsellor can review — not another jobs portal, and not a camera-corridor product.

The national conversation on AI keeps returning to sovereignty, national languages, and public services. We build the missing speech-to-intake desk tool.

The pipe

Audio to a signed record. One loop, many desks.

A statement in the language spoken. A transcript. A structured draft. A person who edits every field. Then export and an audit trail. The schema changes by desk; the review step does not.

  1. 01 · Audio

    A statement, in the language spoken.

  2. 02 · Transcript

    Speech becomes text the reviewer can hear against.

  3. 03 · Structured draft

    AI fills fixed fields. Nothing is filed.

  4. 04 · Human review

    A person edits every field.

  5. 05 · Export & audit

    A durable record. Every step is reconstructable.

One pipe, tenanted per public-service desk — not a claim of ministry adoption.

Voice intake, not a portal

The missing layer is speech to a reviewable form. We do not replace national employment sites, and we do not sell a camera corridor.

National languages, first-class

Somali, Afar, Arabic, and French are the working languages — not an afterthought bolted onto an English-only pipeline.

Officer and counsellor trust

The model drafts. The person at the desk decides. A police incident file and a jobseeker dossier use the same review step.

Skills and local capacity

Built so Djiboutian talent can run the loop — formation and review, not an offshore service that disappears at the end of a contract.

Institutional seriousness

Public-service tempo. Long-horizon records that outlast a project cycle. Quiet competence — not a launch countdown.

About

Led by Radwan Jama.

Phoenix Rising Group is led by Radwan Jama, a software engineer with experience in applied machine learning and large-scale systems (Palo Alto Networks, Google Fellowship, Fidelity). He is building Phoenix Rising Group independently, focused on speech and documentation technology for Somali, Afar, Arabic, French, and other Horn of Africa languages.

The project combines:

  • Voice-intake tools for public-service desks — VoiceDoc, Emploi Assist, and Transport
  • A human-in-the-loop pipe: audio, transcript, structured draft, review, export
  • SASR Eval, a public research yardstick for long-game Somali ASR

Radwan studied Computer Science at Colgate University and has previously worked on ML-powered static analysis, large-scale systems, and AI security tooling.
