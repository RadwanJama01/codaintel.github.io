SASR Eval is a research note on Somali speech recognition. It is not a desk. Methods and scores

How it works

  1. 01 Record You talk at the desk.
  2. 02 Draft You talk. It writes the file.
  3. 03 Review A person reads every line.
  4. 04 Export Marked DRAFT until a person signs off.
Who

Phoenix Rising Group

Phoenix Rising Group turns spoken statements into draft forms for police desks, job centres, and weigh stations. An officer reviews every line before anything is saved.

Nothing saved without a human sign-off. Live demo stage. No ministry has adopted this.
