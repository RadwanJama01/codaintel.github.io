Honest evaluation

Validated on public data. Methods in the open.

SASR Eval is a ground-truth evaluation of commercial and fine-tuned ASR systems on Somali speech. The current result is public-data validation: 50 stitched clips, ~550 seconds total, scored against verified references. Broadcast-domain validation on regional archives is in progress.

What we build

Speech systems for archives and institutions.

The layer the region has been missing between raw speech and a file someone can review, correct, and keep. Policy into actions concrètes — not a black box offshore.

  1. 01 Voice A statement, in the language spoken.
  2. 02 Structured draft AI fills fixed fields. Nothing is filed.
  3. 03 Human review A person edits every field.
  4. 04 Signed record Submit, then a durable PDF.

A product capability for field documentation — not a claim of adoption.

Filing speed and an audit trail

Voice to a structured record, then review, then a signed PDF. Faster filing without silent automation — every step is reconstructable.

National languages, first-class

Somali, Afar, Arabic, and French are the working languages — not an afterthought bolted onto an English-only pipeline.

Honest evaluation

SASR Eval publishes method, clips, and scores. No “best ASR in Africa.” No magic number without a published eval.

Skills and local capacity

Built so Djiboutian talent can run the loop — formation and review, not an offshore service that disappears at the end of a contract.

Institutional seriousness

Public-service tempo. Long-horizon records that outlast a project cycle. Quiet competence — not a launch countdown.

About

Led by Radwan Jama.

Phoenix Rising Group is led by Radwan Jama, a software engineer with experience in applied machine learning and large-scale systems (Palo Alto Networks, Google Fellowship, Fidelity). He is building Phoenix Rising Group independently, focused on speech and documentation technology for Somali, Afar, Arabic, French, and other Horn of Africa languages.

The project combines:

  • Fine-tuned speech recognition, validated in public as SASR Eval
  • Human-in-the-loop tools for annotation and institutional documentation
  • Applied systems designed for public-service records, not unattended transcription

Radwan studied Computer Science at Colgate University and has previously worked on ML-powered static analysis, large-scale systems, and AI security tooling.
