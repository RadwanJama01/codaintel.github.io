Language Technology · Research Stage Somali ASR

Preserving Horn of Africa Languages Through Speech AI

Phoenix Rising Group is building speech technology for the Horn of Africa, starting with high-accuracy Somali ASR. 37.8% lower word error rate than commercial systems.

The work is grounded in regional broadcast and cultural archives, and is being applied to practical systems for documentation and institutional use in Djibouti and the wider region.

What we build

Speech systems for archives and institutions.

High-accuracy Somali ASR is the technical core. The applied work is digitizing Horn of Africa archives and supporting speech-enabled documentation workflows in the region.

Archive digitization

Automated ingestion and transcription of regional broadcast media, oral history, and cultural programming for long-term preservation.

Domain-specific ASR

Fine-tuned Whisper-small, trained in ~1 hour on a single T4 GPU. Outperforms commercial systems on the current Somali evaluation set.

Applied documentation systems

Speech-enabled workflows for institutional documentation and archival use in Djibouti and the wider Horn of Africa.

Products

Annotation and field documentation tools.

Voice-driven documentation and language technology built for the Horn of Africa. Each product keeps a person in the loop: AI drafts, a human reviews.

Annotation platform · Somali & Afar

Horn Transcribe

An in-house workspace where AI drafts Somali and Afar broadcast transcripts and a human transcriber corrects them — the workflow behind SASR Eval, built into a tool.
Voice documentation · Field agencies

About

Led by Radwan Jama.

Phoenix Rising Group is led by Radwan Jama, a software engineer with experience in applied machine learning and large-scale systems (Palo Alto Networks, Google Fellowship, Fidelity). He is building Phoenix Rising Group independently, focused on speech technology for Somali and other Horn of Africa languages.

The project combines:

  • Fine-tuned speech recognition validated against commercial systems
  • Work on regional broadcast and cultural archives
  • Applied systems for institutional documentation use cases

Radwan studied Computer Science at Colgate University and has previously worked on ML-powered static analysis, large-scale systems, and AI security tooling.
