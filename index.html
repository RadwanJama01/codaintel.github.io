What we do

Language preservation through speech AI.

Phoenix Rising Group is building a platform to process and preserve broadcast, oral history, and cultural archives from Djibouti, Somaliland, and the broader Horn of Africa.

Archive digitization

Automated ingestion and transcription of regional broadcast media, oral history, and cultural programming for long-term preservation.

Domain-specific ASR

Our fine-tuned pipeline outperforms commercial systems on Somali speech. Trained in ~1 hour on a single T4 GPU with 500 steps.

Regional access

Father-son team with deep cultural and political ties to Djibouti and Somaliland. Local relationships enable data collection and community partnerships others cannot replicate.
