About

Capabilities

Persistent, low-cost broadcast OSINT from a region where intelligence coverage is thin, backed by validated ASR and deep regional ties.

Mission

Broadcast intelligence from the Horn of Africa.

Coda Labs processes regional broadcast media to provide open-source intelligence for national security applications: threat detection, propaganda monitoring, and early warning. We operate at TRL 3: proof-of-concept validated on public data, with broadcast validation underway.

What we do

Persistent, low-cost broadcast OSINT from the Horn of Africa. Automated pipelines ingest, transcribe, and analyze regional media at scale.

Data

Building a platform to process broadcast data from a strategically critical region. Decades of regional media spanning news, interviews, and cultural programming.

Technology

Our fine-tuned ASR pipeline outperforms commercial systems. SASR Eval achieved 37.8% lower WER than ElevenLabs and 57.8% lower than Whisper large-v3.

Technology

Fine-tuned ASR pipeline.

Our fine-tuned ASR pipeline has been validated on Somali speech. SASR Eval achieved 37.8% lower WER than ElevenLabs and 57.8% lower than Whisper large-v3. We’re now applying this to the Horn of Africa’s broadcast ecosystem.

Data platform

Building for Horn of Africa broadcast intelligence.

We are building a platform to process and analyze decades of broadcast data from the Horn of Africa including news, interviews, cultural and educational programming in Somali and regional languages. This is the foundation for persistent OSINT at a fraction of traditional collection costs.

Regional broadcast coverage

Platform designed for continuous ingestion of Horn of Africa media from Djibouti, Somaliland, and adjacent territories where commercial intelligence products have limited reach.

Archive processing pipeline

Infrastructure to digitize, transcribe, and index historical broadcast recordings. Enables retrospective analysis alongside real-time monitoring.

Partner briefing available

Detailed data access terms, archive specifications, and collection methodology are shared with qualified defense and intelligence partners under NDA. Contact us to request a briefing.

Competitive positioning

Commercial ASR systems underperform on Somali (WER 0.59–0.87 on public benchmarks). Our fine-tuned model at WER 0.368 demonstrates that domain-specific training closes this gap.

Public disclosure policy. We do not publicly claim exclusive data access until agreements are fully executed. General capabilities are described here; specific archive details are available in partner briefings only.
Team

Deep regional ties.

Coda Labs is a father-son team with deep cultural and political ties to Djibouti and Somaliland. This is not a remote research effort. It is built on relationships with regional broadcasters and language communities.

Regional expertise

Native understanding of Horn of Africa politics, dialects, and media landscapes. Relationships with broadcast institutions in Djibouti and Somaliland.

Technical capability

Computer science background with hands-on ASR development. Built and validated SASR Eval end-to-end: dataset construction, model training, and benchmark scoring.

Defense engagement

Actively engaging with DIU, SOFWERX, AFWERX, and the Army’s JIOP. Seeking transition partners for broadcast OSINT at scale.
