Capabilities

Speech technology for the Horn of Africa

High-accuracy Somali ASR, grounded in regional archives, applied to documentation and institutional systems. Led by Radwan Jama.

What we build

One technical core, three applications.

Phoenix Rising Group is building speech technology for the Horn of Africa, starting with Somali ASR. Public-data validation is complete. Broadcast-domain validation is in progress.

Language preservation

Persistent, low-cost processing of broadcast, oral history, and cultural archives so Somali, Afar, and other regional languages are represented in the systems being built today.

Domain-specific ASR

Fine-tuned Whisper-small, trained in ~1 hour on a single T4 GPU. SASR Eval: 37.8% lower WER than ElevenLabs and 57.8% lower than Whisper large-v3 on public Somali speech data.

Applied documentation systems

Speech-enabled workflows for archival and institutional documentation in Djibouti and the wider region. Built on the same ASR stack.

Technology

Fine-tuned Whisper-small, publicly evaluated.

Current result is public-data validation on 50 clips (~550 seconds). Broadcast-domain validation on regional archives is in progress. Full SASR Eval results.

Data platform

Built for Horn of Africa broadcast and archives.

We are building infrastructure to process decades of broadcast data from the Horn of Africa including news, interviews, and cultural programming in Somali and regional languages. That archive work is also the training and evaluation path for applied documentation systems.

Regional broadcast coverage

Designed for continuous ingestion of Horn of Africa media from Djibouti, Somaliland, and adjacent territories where language technology has limited reach.

Archive processing pipeline

Infrastructure to digitize, transcribe, and index historical broadcast recordings. Enables retrospective analysis alongside ongoing archival work.

Partner briefing available

Detailed data access terms, archive specifications, and collection methodology are shared with qualified research and institutional partners under NDA.

Measured gap vs. commercial ASR

Commercial systems underperform on Somali (WER 0.59–0.87 on the public evaluation set). Fine-tuned Whisper-small at WER 0.368 is the current public-data result.

Public disclosure policy. We do not publicly claim exclusive data access until agreements are fully executed. General capabilities are described here; specific archive details are available in partner briefings only.
About

Led by Radwan Jama.

Phoenix Rising Group is led by Radwan Jama, a software engineer with experience in applied machine learning and large-scale systems (Palo Alto Networks, Google Fellowship, Fidelity). He is building Phoenix Rising Group independently, focused on speech technology for Somali and other Horn of Africa languages.

The project combines:

  • Fine-tuned speech recognition validated against commercial systems
  • Work on regional broadcast and cultural archives
  • Applied systems for institutional documentation use cases

Radwan holds a B.A. in Computer Science from Colgate University and has previously worked on ML-powered static analysis, large-scale systems, and AI security tooling. He built and validated SASR Eval end-to-end: dataset construction, model training, and benchmark scoring.
