Capabilities

Speech to a reviewable form

The language layer for Djibouti and Horn of Africa public-service last mile: spoken Somali, Afar, Arabic, and French become official-style drafts, with a human in the loop. Led by Radwan Jama.

What we build

One pipe. Live desks. A long-game yardstick.

Phoenix Rising Group builds speech and documentation AI for the Horn of Africa. The live work is voice intake for public-service desks. SASR Eval stays published as a research yardstick for Somali ASR — not a product push.

Voice intake

Spoken Somali, Afar, Arabic, and French become a structured draft an officer or counsellor can review. VoiceDoc and Emploi Assist are the live desks.

Human-in-the-loop pipe

Audio, transcript, structured draft, human review, export and audit. The schema changes by desk; the review step does not.

National languages, first-class

Working languages are Somali, Afar, Arabic, and French — not an afterthought bolted onto an English-only pipeline.

Technology

SASR Eval: a research yardstick, not a live product.

Public-data validation on 50 clips (~550 seconds) stays up as a long-game Somali ASR yardstick. The ASR lab track is frozen. Scores are not a homepage product claim. Full SASR Eval results.

Data platform

Built for Horn of Africa broadcast and archives.

We are building infrastructure to process decades of broadcast data from the Horn of Africa including news, interviews, and cultural programming in Somali and regional languages. Archive work stays on the annotation side of the house; live desks are voice intake.

Regional broadcast coverage

Designed for continuous ingestion of Horn of Africa media from Djibouti, Somaliland, and adjacent territories where language technology has limited reach.

Archive processing pipeline

Infrastructure to digitize, transcribe, and index historical broadcast recordings. Enables retrospective analysis alongside ongoing archival work.

Partner details on request

Detailed data access terms, archive specifications, and collection methodology are shared with qualified research and institutional partners under NDA.

Measured gap vs. commercial ASR

Commercial systems underperform on Somali (WER 0.59–0.87 on the public evaluation set). Fine-tuned Whisper-small at WER 0.368 is the current public-data result.

Public disclosure policy. We do not publicly claim exclusive data access until agreements are fully executed. General capabilities are described here; specific archive details are shared with qualified partners only.
About

Led by Radwan Jama.

Phoenix Rising Group is led by Radwan Jama, a software engineer with experience in applied machine learning and large-scale systems (Palo Alto Networks, Google Fellowship, Fidelity). He is building Phoenix Rising Group independently, focused on speech technology for Somali and other Horn of Africa languages.

The project combines:

  • Voice-intake tools for public-service desks — VoiceDoc and Emploi Assist
  • A human-in-the-loop pipe for institutional documentation
  • SASR Eval as a public research yardstick for long-game Somali ASR

Radwan studied Computer Science at Colgate University and has previously worked on ML-powered static analysis, large-scale systems, and AI security tooling. He built and validated SASR Eval end-to-end: dataset construction, model training, and benchmark scoring.
