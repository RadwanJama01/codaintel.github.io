Capabilities

One platform for the work behind every decision.

From the moment a file arrives to the moment someone signs off, we take on the reading, checking and retyping so your team can focus on judgment.

What it does

Four jobs, one record.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Applications, forms and documents arrive from paper, scans, photos or the web, in the languages your teams work in.

  2. 02

    Verification

    Every document is read and checked against your rules and against the rest of the file. What is missing or inconsistent is flagged.

  3. 03

    Review

    Each result reaches the right person with the issues already marked, so reviewers spend their time deciding, not retyping.

  4. 04

    Records

    A clear trail of what was submitted, what was checked and who signed off.

How it is delivered

Delivered as a service, with people behind it.

We configure each deployment around one workflow, train the staff who use it and stay with it after launch. It runs on the web, with mobile access for teams in the field.
